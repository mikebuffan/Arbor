## REVIEW-001 — count-slip mismatch (already explained in published investigation)

- Source locator: OIG-23-085, **PDF page 73, printed page 66**, paragraphs 2–4.
- Documents described: the August 10 SHU midnight count slip said **73**, while 3 a.m. and 5 a.m. slips said **72**.
- OIG conclusion: the midnight slip was inaccurate; an inmate had already been transferred out of the SHU; the later slips listed 72. The OIG also found the counts were not physically conducted despite signed paperwork.
- Classification: **published discrepancy with an attributed explanation**, not a newly unresolved contradiction. Do not suggest a change in population based only on the numbers.

## REVIEW-002 — reported instructions for August 9 telephone call

- Source locators: OIG-23-085, **PDF page 65, printed 58** (Captain and Unit Manager accounts); **PDF page 66, printed 59** (Unit Manager call account).
- Captain's reported account: he told the Unit Manager a call could happen, **but it had to be monitored and logged**.
- Unit Manager's reported account: he did not recall that conversation and said he chose to allow the call. He acknowledged the call was not monitored and that he did not verify the recipient.
- Classification: **difference between attributed witness recollections; previously disclosed in the OIG report**. The record does not establish what was said in the conversation or anything about the reason for Epstein's death. Further comparison should seek contemporaneous dated BOP logs, interview transcripts and source-page images, not amplify inference from a single report.

## TRIAGE-003 — chronology/index checks, NOT verified discrepancy

- DOJ search-indexed text for EFTA00034505 gives the social call at about **7:00 p.m.** August 9 and the body alarm at **6:33 a.m.** August 10. The OIG reports the call from **6:58–7:19 p.m.**, **PDF page 65, printed 58**. These times appear compatible, not contradictory.
- The indexed timeline lists a **7:20 a.m.** notification after a **7:36 a.m.** time-of-death entry; this may be nonchronological ordering. **Do not present as a factual contradiction** unless the original image is checked, the event definitions are aligned, and any later corrections examined.
